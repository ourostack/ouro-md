# Lantern Night: opening plan

**Drafted by** your agent · **Reviewed by** Maya · **Status:** ready for a second read

One evening on Harbor Street, twelve stalls, and every window lit. This plan keeps the first night small enough to get right.

## The evening at a glance

| Time | What happens | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5:30 | Stalls set up, lanterns hung | Volunteers |
| 6:30 | Gates open, music starts | Jun and the band |
| 8:00 | Lantern walk along the water | Everyone |
| 9:30 | Lights down, tables away | Last ones out |

## Invitation

Come down to Harbor Street on Friday night. Bring a lantern if you have one; we will have spares at the gate.

## Decisions

- Twelve stalls, not twenty. Room to walk beats room to sell.
- Paper lanterns only along the water, battery lights everywhere else.
- One quiet corner with chairs and no speakers.

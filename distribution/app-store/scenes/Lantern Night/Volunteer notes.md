# Volunteer notes

## Lanterns

- Hang lanterns at shoulder height so faces stay lit.
- Every lantern along the water is paper, so keep a bucket of sand at each post.
- Count the spare lanterns at the gate before 6:00.

## The lantern walk

Start at stall 12, follow the railing to the lighthouse, and come back along the market side. Walk slowly; the point is to look at the water.

## If something goes wrong

Find Jun at the gate, or Maya by the tea stall.

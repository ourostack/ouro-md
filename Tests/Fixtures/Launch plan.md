# Harbor launch plan

**Objective:** Open the fictional harbor garden with a small, welcoming pilot.

## Invitation

Join us for the garden opening. Take a short walk, share a cup of tea, and tell us what would make your next visit easier.

## Feedback

Make the first invitation clearer.

## Decisions

- Begin with twelve visitors, not a broad launch.
- Keep the invitation to two friendly sentences.
- Put a person at the gate to welcome arrivals.
